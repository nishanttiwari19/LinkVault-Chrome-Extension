How to test LinkVault on this form:
  1. Click the LinkVault extension icon in your Chrome toolbar.
  2. Make sure your profile links (LinkedIn, GitHub, Website, Portfolio, Resume) are configured.
  3. Click "Autofill Current Page" in the LinkVault popup.
  4. Watch the matching fields fill instantly with glowing blue feedback!
  5. Notice that sensitive fields (Password, Security code) and unrelated fields (Name, Email) are safely skipped!
