How LinkVault Matches Fields

  • LinkedIn: Scans for labels or placeholders containing "LinkedIn", "LinkedIn URL", "LinkedIn Profile".
  • GitHub: Scans for "GitHub", "Git Profile", "GitHub URL", "Repository".
  • Portfolio: Scans for "Portfolio", "Portfolio Website", "Work Samples", "Projects URL".
  • Resume: Scans for "Resume", "CV", "Curriculum Vitae", "Resume URL", "Drive URL".
  • Personal Website: Matches generic "Website", "Homepage", "Personal Site" ONLY if specific platform fields are not specified.
  • Safety Guarantee: Sensitive fields (passwords, credit cards, SSN, OTP, hidden inputs) are strictly ignored and never filled. Unknown fields are never filled with guesses.
